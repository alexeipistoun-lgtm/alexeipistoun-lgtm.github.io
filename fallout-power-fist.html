Fallout Power Fist

A real-life wearable mechanism inspired by the Power Fist from Fallout, built around two pneumatic cylinders and a welded and 3D-printed frame.

← Back to all projects

Project Overview

I built the Power Fist as a real-life interpretation of the wearable weapon from Fallout. The mechanism used two pneumatic cylinders mounted to a welded frame with 3D-printed components forming parts of the wearable structure.

The project went through several revisions. During testing, the mechanism was hard on its own structure and welds failed more than once. I later added springs to damp the motion and continued modifying the frame before finishing and painting a later version.

Engineering Story

  • Started with a CAD concept for the wearable frame.
  • Fabricated and welded the structural frame.
  • Integrated two pneumatic cylinders, tubing, valves, and electrical connections.
  • Used 3D-printed parts as part of the wearable structure and enclosure geometry.
  • Observed structural failures during testing and redesigned the mechanism.
  • Added springs in a later revision to reduce the impact the mechanism placed on itself.
