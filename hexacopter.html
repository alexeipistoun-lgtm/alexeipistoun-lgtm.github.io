Project Overview

This project explored the integration of a manually flyable hexacopter with onboard computing and depth sensing. The aircraft flew manually, while the computer-vision and autonomous-flight portion remained an experimental, unfinished part of the project.

What I worked on

  • Built and configured a six-motor carbon-fiber hexacopter.
  • Integrated a Pixhawk 6 flight controller.
  • Added an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano and Intel RealSense depth camera for onboard perception experiments.
  • Experimented with Python and OpenCV for computer vision.
  • Achieved manual flight; autonomous computer-vision control was not completed successfully.
