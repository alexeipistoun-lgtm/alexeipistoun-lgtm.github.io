SteakyBone

An injection-molded nylon dog chew I developed from concept through tooling, manufacturing, production documentation, and sales.

← Back to all projects

Project Overview

SteakyBone is a physical consumer product built around a T-bone-shaped nylon dog chew. I developed the product through repeated mold, part, and process iterations and built the manufacturing workflow around a Morgan G100 injection molding press.

What I worked on

  • Designed and iterated four aluminum injection molds with attention to mold filling, manufacturability, and part strength.
  • Developed the material-processing and molding workflow for repeatable production.
  • Created setup, processing, and production documentation.
  • Produced more than 400 parts across mold iterations and sold more than 100 units.
