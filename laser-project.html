Project Overview

Integrating controls, electronics, and a printed mechanical package.

This project was an experimental hardware build focused on integrating control electronics with a compact 3D-printed mechanical assembly. I worked through bench testing, wiring, packaging, and repeated physical tests while refining the prototype.

The project is included as an example of hands-on electronics integration and rapid prototyping rather than as a finished product.

What I worked on

  • Bench-tested control electronics and supporting hardware.
  • Integrated electrical components into a compact prototype assembly.
  • Designed and fabricated 3D-printed mechanical packaging.
  • Iterated the physical build based on test results.
