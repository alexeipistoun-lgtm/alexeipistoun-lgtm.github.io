WoofWatch

A student engineering venture developing an anti-theft smart dog collar with custom electronics, embedded hardware, and mechanical enclosure development.

← Back to all projects

Project Overview

WoofWatch combined electronics and mechanical product development in a collar intended to provide tracking and a locking mechanism. The project progressed through multiple PCB and enclosure revisions and was developed as a student engineering venture.

What I worked on

  • Led electrical development for a five-person team.
  • Designed four PCB revisions using Altium and EasyEDA.
  • Developed and iterated eight SolidWorks housing designs.
  • Helped develop the project through Aggies Create, including competition and award milestones.
