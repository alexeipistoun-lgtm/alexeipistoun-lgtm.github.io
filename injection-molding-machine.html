Project Overview

I built and modified a small injection molding machine to support larger molded parts than the original Buster Beagle-style configuration. The redesign focused on increasing usable shot capacity and improving thermal control across the molding process.

I left the previously listed 1,770 lbf force figure off this page for now so it can be verified before publishing it as a technical specification.

What I worked on

  • Expanded the machine's shot capacity from approximately 56 g to approximately 93 g.
  • Implemented multi-zone PID temperature control for the barrel, nozzle, and mold.
  • Worked through mechanical fabrication, assembly, heating, and process-development challenges.
  • Used the machine as part of hands-on injection molding and product-development work.
