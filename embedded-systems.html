Project Overview

My embedded-systems coursework includes microcontroller programming, GPIO control, FPGA logic development, and the division of system behavior between firmware and programmable logic.

What I worked on

  • Developed STM32 firmware in C using Keil and HAL-based GPIO control.
  • Implemented digital logic in FPGA development environments including Vivado and Quartus.
  • Worked with embedded C/C++, ARM assembly, and hardware/software interfaces.
  • Built experience debugging systems where behavior is split between firmware and FPGA logic.
